About Us

Over a decade of honest craftsmanship in Mangalore

Founded in 2015 by Ivan Dsouza, Zain Fabrication began with a simple promise — to deliver metal work that is strong, precise and finished with pride. What started as a small workshop has grown into a trusted name for residential, commercial and industrial fabrication across Mangalore.

Every project is personally overseen, from the first measurement to the final coat of paint. We combine quality materials, skilled welding and practical design to build work that looks great and stands the test of coastal weather.

  • Skilled, experienced team
  • Rust-resistant finishing
  • On-time delivery
